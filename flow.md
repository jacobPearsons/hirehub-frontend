· Goal: To assess fit and provide a positive candidate experience.
· Tool: Email Invitation through Microsoft Teams
· Process:
  1. Invitation: Send a personalized email inviting the candidate to an interview (phone, video, or in-person). Include the interviewer's name and a link to schedule.
  2. Confirmation: Once scheduled, send a calendar invite with all details (link, phone number, address).
  3. Practice Portal: Provide access to the secure candidate portal for interview practice. This is a great tool to help candidates prepare, as shown in your screenshot of Raymond Gonzales's completed practice session.

Stage 5: First Day Orientation & Onboarding

· Goal: To officially welcome the new hire, finalize paperwork, and integrate them into the team.
· Tool: In-Person or Virtual Meeting.
· Process (Based on your text):
  · A formal orientation will be conducted prior to the start of operations.
  · Agenda:
    1. Welcome and introductions to the team.
    2. Review of PATHMATCH's mission, history, and values in depth.
    3. Progress Assessment: A review of the pre-boarding tasks and preliminary duties (office setup, hardware).
    4. Paperwork Finalization: Signing any remaining employment forms.
    5. IT Setup: Provision of login credentials, email, and necessary software.
    6. Review of first-week goals and expectations.

This structured process ensures no step is missed, reduces new hire anxiety, and professionally represents the PATHMATCH brand from the very first interaction.

# HireHub Community — Visual Context

> Warm community-driven hiring platform. Orange #ff5600 accent on warm neutral #f5f1ec canvas. Professional but approachable, editorial yet human.

---

## Brand DNA

```
Brand:       HireHub Community — connecting talent with opportunity
Personality: Warm, professional, approachable, community-driven, human-centered
Palette:     Canvas #f5f1ec | Surface-1 #ffffff | Surface-2 #ebe7e1
             Ink #111111 | Ink-muted #626260 | Ink-tertiary #9c9fa5

```

soft diffused shadows, 3% grain overlay, generous negative space (30-40%),
authentic diverse subjects in genuine postures, no stock-photo tropes

## Photography Rules

### Do's
- Natural light always — golden hour preferred, soft window light acceptable
- Authentic subjects in genuine postures — mid-conversation, mid-discovery, mid-collaboration
- Warm color grading — slight warmth in highlights, neutral shadows
- 30-40% negative space in every composition for text overlay
- Diverse subjects in age, ethnicity, and gender presentation
- Casual-professional attire — open collars, rolled sleeves, no ties
- Real environments — co-working spaces, home offices, coffee shops
- 3% grain overlay for editorial warmth
- 35-50mm lens range — natural perspective, no distortion

### Don'ts
- No stock-photo tropes — no handshakes, no fake smiles, no forced eye contact
- No fluorescent or cold blue lighting
- No sterile corporate offices — always show warmth and life
- No plastic skin or heavy retouching
- No text, logos, or watermarks in images
- No oversaturation — keep colors true to the brand palette
- No 3D renders or CGI-looking imagery
- No busy backgrounds — keep focus on subjects

### Color Matching
All photography should be color-graded to match the brand palette:
- Highlights: warm cream (#F5F0E8 tint)
- Midtones: neutral with slight warmth
- Shadows: cool-neutral (no blue cast)
- Skin tones: natural, warm, true-to-life
- Accent pops: #FF5600 should appear naturally in clothing, objects, or environment

### Image Generation Checklist
When generating images for any section:
- [ ] Matches the section's emotional objective
- [ ] Follows Photography DNA (lens, lighting, composition)
- [ ] Uses brand palette colors in the scene
- [ ] Has 30-40% negative space for text overlay
- [ ] Includes dark overlay at 40% opacity where text sits (WCAG AA)
- [ ] No negative items (stock tropes, cold tones, etc.)
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio for placement (16:9 hero, 9:16 mobile, 1:1 icons)

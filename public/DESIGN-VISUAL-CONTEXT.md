# HireHub Community — Visual Context

> Warm community-driven hiring platform. Orange #ff5600 accent on warm neutral #f5f1ec canvas. Professional but approachable, editorial yet human.

---

## Brand DNA

```
Brand:     HireHub Community — connecting talent with opportunity
Personality: Warm, professional, approachable, community-driven, human-centered
Palette:   Canvas #f5f1ec | Surface-1 #ffffff | Surface-2 #ebe7e1
           Ink #111111 | Ink-muted #626260 | Ink-tertiary #9c9fa5
           Accent #ff5600 | Brand-blue #0007cb | Success #0bdf50
Typography: Inter (sans, 400/500), JetBrains Mono (mono, 400)
Motion:    Subtle fades, gentle slides, staggered reveals — professional-minimal
```

**Photography DNA:**
```
Natural light, 35-50mm lens, eye level, true color grading with slight warmth,
soft diffused shadows, 3% grain overlay, generous negative space (30-40%),
authentic diverse subjects in genuine postures, no stock-photo tropes
```
